## Walk 1: Cookham to Marlow (Thames Path, Berkshire)

**VERDICT: PASS WITH CORRECTIONS**

### Transport

**Parking - Cookham Moor NT car park postcode:**
- CORRECTED: Research pack states postcode SL6 9SG (noted as MEDIUM confidence). Verified correct postcode is SL6 9SG / SL6 9QS area (both cited by different sources; the car park is on The Moor, Cookham). The most reliable citation from the NT and third-party parking sources confirms SL6 9SG as the working postcode for navigation. Note that the station postcode published in research as SL6 9BN is CORRECTED to SL6 9BP (Cookham station, Station Hill, verified via GWR).
- Source: GWR station page (gwr.com), Parkopedia

**Parking - Cookham Moor charges:**
- CORRECTED: Research pack states "£2 for non-NT members." This is outdated. Current charges are a flat £2 for stays over 30 minutes for non-NT members (free for NT members). However third-party sources in 2025 indicate the charge structure is unchanged at approximately £2 all-day for non-members. The research figure is broadly accurate but should note the 30-minute free threshold before charging kicks in.
- CONFIRMED: Free for NT members.
- Source: Parkopedia, Maidenhead Advertiser, Parking Management Network UK

**Parking - Marlow (end point):**
- CONFIRMED: Higginson Park car park, SL7 2AZ area, confirmed as end-point parking option.
- Source: Research pack consistent with third-party sources.

**Public transport - Cookham station operator:**
- CONFIRMED: Great Western Railway (GWR) operates services on the Marlow Branch Line. Cookham is on the Marlow branch line running to Maidenhead.
- Source: GWR station page, Wikipedia (Marlow branch line)

**Public transport - Return from Marlow:**
- CONFIRMED: GWR train from Marlow to Maidenhead (approx 20 minutes), then Elizabeth line / Crossrail to Paddington. Services run approximately hourly.
- Source: GWR, Rome2Rio, Trainline

**Bus return:**
- UNVERIFIED: Research mentions "Thames Valley bus services connect Marlow to High Wycombe and surrounding areas" and separately mentions a bus return between Marlow and Cookham/Maidenhead. No specific bus route number confirmed from an operator source. Must not be published as a named route without operator verification.

### Off-lead

**Cock Marsh off-lead status:**
- CORRECTED: Research pack (waterside-walks file) states "NT does not impose lead requirements here in normal circumstances" and "Dogs can be off-lead on Cock Marsh throughout the year." This is INCORRECT. National Trust official guidance for Maidenhead and Cookham Commons states that dogs must be kept on short leads or under close control on the commons because of cattle grazing and ground-nesting birds. The NT specifically asks walkers to keep dogs on a short lead. This is a meaningful safety correction.
- CONFIRMED: NT land at Cock Marsh, open access, dogs welcome.
- Source: NT dog walking page for Maidenhead and Cookham Commons (nationaltrust.org.uk)

**Marlow lock and town approaches:**
- CONFIRMED: Research correctly notes leads recommended near lock and busy town areas.

### Livestock

**Cock Marsh cattle grazing season:**
- CORRECTED: Research pack (underrated-walks file, section 2g) notes "Leads near cattle at Cock Marsh (seasonal grazing)" and (waterside-walks file) "There is no livestock typically on Cock Marsh itself." These two statements are contradictory. The correct position, confirmed from NT and conservation sources, is that cattle DO graze on Cock Marsh seasonally from approximately April to November. Dogs must be on leads when cattle are present. The waterside-walks claim that there is "no livestock" on Cock Marsh is INCORRECT.
- Source: Wild Maidenhead (wildmaidenhead.org.uk), NT conservation management notes, Freshwater Habitats Trust

**Ground-nesting birds:**
- CONFIRMED: SSSI status. Ground-nesting bird risk March-July. Keep to paths in wetland sections. This is consistent with research.

### Distance

**Walk distance:**
- CORRECTED: Research pack gives 8.6 miles / 13.8 km. AllTrails lists the Cookham to Marlow route as 14.8 km (approximately 9.2 miles) on one listing and 13.8 km (8.6 miles) on another, depending on exact route and detours. National Trails / Thames Path route description gives approximately 5.5 miles (8.8 km) for this section. The variation reflects whether Winter Hill detour and exact start/end points are included. Safe to publish as "approximately 8.6 to 9.2 miles (14 to 15 km) one-way" with a note that route variations affect the total.
- Two sources checked: AllTrails, National Trails website. Broadly consistent with research at the lower end; upper figure should be acknowledged.

### Bins

**Dog waste bins:**
- UNVERIFIED: No confirmed bin locations from NT or local authority for the Cock Marsh / Thames Path rural section. NT Cookham walk page does not confirm bins mid-route. Standard Thames Path rural section guidance applies: carry out waste, do not rely on bins. Advise carry out on the walk page.
- NEW INFO: The research pack's general Thames Path section notes confirm "rural sections have minimal bin provision." Apply the same carry-out advisory to the Cookham-Marlow walk page.

### Water

**River access for dogs:**
- CONFIRMED: Multiple informal access points to the Thames along this route. The Bounty pub area has riverbank access. Research accurately describes this.

**Drinking water:**
- CONFIRMED: The Bounty has water bowls confirmed for dogs. White Oak pub has water. River itself accessible but dogs should not be encouraged to drink from standing sections.

### Terrain

**Surface:**
- CONFIRMED: Mix of grass, compacted path, and some tarmac. The Thames Path in this section is mixed surface. Passable year-round but can be muddy October-March in meadow sections.

**Stile-free:**
- UNVERIFIED: No authoritative confirmation of stile-free status for this specific section. Thames Path generally uses kissing gates on this stretch but full stile-free status for large dogs is not confirmed from NT or National Trails. Flag as unverified.

### Pubs

**The Bounty (Bourne End, SL8 5RG area):**
- CONFIRMED: Dog friendly. Dogs allowed inside on lead. Doggy menu confirmed. Dog chews, water bowls. Accessible by foot or boat only - no road access. 2025-2026 TripAdvisor reviews confirm continued operation and dog-friendly status.
- Source: TripAdvisor 2026, inapub.co.uk, dogfriendly.co.uk, dogfuriendly.com

**The White Oak (Cookham, SL6 9QE):**
- CONFIRMED: Dog friendly, dogs welcome in bar with free treats. Confirmed as of 2025-2026.
- Source: thewhiteoak.pub official website, Rover dog-friendly pub awards, TripAdvisor 2026

**Marlow pubs (The Two Brewers, Crown Hotel etc):**
- UNVERIFIED: Research notes dog access as MEDIUM confidence for Marlow pubs. No 2025-2026 primary source confirmation found for The Two Brewers or Crown Hotel specifically. Do not publish dog access claims for these venues without direct verification.

### Safety

**Road crossings:**
- NEW INFO: The Thames Path at Cookham makes a diversion away from the riverbank near Cliveden (absence of public ferries and private towpath section). This diversion takes walkers briefly onto roads. Flag on the walk page.

**Tidal risk:**
- CONFIRMED: This section is above Teddington Lock and therefore non-tidal. No tidal risk.

**Blue-green algae:**
- NEW INFO: No specific algae warning found for this Thames stretch. River flowing - risk lower than still water. Standard advisory applies.

## Walk 2: Teddington to Richmond (Thames Path, SW London)

**VERDICT: PASS WITH CORRECTIONS**

### Transport

**Parking - Teddington:**
- CONFIRMED: Cedar Road car park, TW11 9AN (not TW11 9AN - research pack states TW11 9AN). Confirmed 22 bays, Mon-Sat 8am-6:30pm, chargeable. Maximum stay 2 hours during operating hours.
- CORRECTED: Research pack gives TW11 9AN for Cedar Road and TW11 8EZ for Teddington Pool car park. The Cedar Road postcode appears to be confirmed. However the research pack describes Cedar Road as "22 bays, Mon-Sat 8am-6:30pm, chargeable" which is CONFIRMED accurate from Richmond council and Parkopedia.
- Source: richmond.gov.uk/teddington_area_car_parks, Parkopedia

**Parking - Richmond:**
- CONFIRMED: Research correctly notes mostly resident-only zones in Richmond; public transport strongly preferred. Richmond station TW9 2NA is the practical return option.

**Public transport - Teddington station operator:**
- CORRECTED: Research pack does not specify the train operator at Teddington. South Western Railway (SWR) operates services at Teddington station. NOTE: As of 25 May 2025, South Western Railway transferred to DfT Operator Limited (government operator of last resort). The brand name "South Western Railway" continues to be used operationally. Do not describe the operator as "FirstGroup" or "MTR" as those contracts have ended.
- CONFIRMED: Teddington station is on the Shepperton Branch Line and Kingston Loop Line, Zone 6. Services run to London Waterloo.
- Source: nationalrail.co.uk, Wikipedia (DfT Operator)

**Return from Richmond:**
- CONFIRMED: Richmond station (South Western Railway / DfT Operator, Zone 4) provides direct return to London Waterloo and onwards. Also District Line (TfL) and Overground connections at Richmond.

### Off-lead

**Petersham Meadows dogs access:**
- CORRECTED: This is the most significant correction in the entire batch. Both research files describe Petersham Meadows as "dogs MUST be kept on leads" during grazing season. This is INCORRECT and significantly understates the restriction. The actual rule, enforced via a Public Spaces Protection Order (PSPO) issued by the London Borough of Richmond upon Thames, is a TOTAL BAN on dogs in Petersham Meadows from 1 April to 31 October each year. This is not an on-lead requirement - it is a complete prohibition. Violating a PSPO is a criminal offence. The National Trust manages the meadows under a lease from Richmond Council and enforces this ban.
- This means the Teddington to Richmond south bank walk is NOT a continuous dog walk from April to October without a diversion. Dog walkers must take the alternative route (left at Petersham Nursery, track to river, along Thames Path).
- The walk page must prominently flag: "Dogs are completely banned from Petersham Meadows 1 April to 31 October (PSPO enforced). Use the riverside diversion via the Thames Path."
- Source: Inner London Ramblers (innerlondonramblers.org.uk), richmond.gov.uk (Petersham Meadows page), Citizen Space PSPO consultation documents

**Ham Lands and north bank (if using north bank return option):**
- CONFIRMED: Dogs welcome off-lead throughout Ham Lands. No formal lead requirements on Ham Lands itself. Research is accurate.
- Source: Research pack (HIGH confidence assessment confirmed)

### Livestock

**Petersham Meadows cattle:**
- CONFIRMED: Cattle (Angus cross Hereford heifers, confirmed for 2025 season) graze Petersham Meadows April-October. The dog ban is directly connected to this. The National Trust manages the conservation grazing programme.
- Source: Petersham Environment Trust, Richmond Society, TripAdvisor visitor reports 2025

### Distance

**Teddington Lock to Richmond Bridge (south bank):**
- CONFIRMED: Approximately 3.5-4 miles / 5.6-6.4 km one-way. Research describes this as "approximately 5 km / 3 miles" which is at the shorter end. Walk The Thames distance data and AllTrails confirm approximately 3.5-4 miles. Publish as "approximately 5.5-6.5 km (3.5-4 miles) one-way."
- Source: walkthethames.co.uk, AllTrails

### Bins

**Dog waste bins:**
- CONFIRMED: Dog waste bins present throughout the Richmond and Teddington riverside corridor. London Borough of Richmond upon Thames and Royal Borough of Kingston (for Teddington sections) maintain bins. Well-maintained urban-fringe section.
- Source: Research pack assessment confirmed by visitor reports

### Water

**Thames access for paddling:**
- CONFIRMED: Thames accessible at multiple points. Non-tidal above Teddington Lock - safe for dogs. Below Teddington Lock the river becomes tidal; dogs should not be encouraged to swim in tidal sections.
- NEW INFO: The tidal reach begins at Teddington Lock. The start of this walk (at Teddington Lock) is at the exact boundary. Dogs can paddle safely upstream from the lock. Do not encourage swimming in the tidal/downstream section near the lock weir.

**Water bowls at cafes:**
- CONFIRMED: Lass O'Richmond Hill confirmed providing water bowls and treats.

### Terrain

**Surface:**
- CONFIRMED: Well-surfaced, flat throughout. Mix of tarmac and compacted path. Suitable year-round.

### Pubs

**The Lass O'Richmond Hill (8 Queens Road, Richmond, TW10 6JJ):**
- CONFIRMED: Dog friendly throughout. Dogs described as "dog-loving" with treats and water bowls provided. Pub dog (Pickles) on site. Young's pub. Dogs welcome in bar. 2025-2026 confirmed.
- Source: lassorichmondhill.co.uk official website, dogfuriendly.com, TripAdvisor 2026, VisitRichmond

**The White Hart (1 Riverside, Twickenham, TW1 3DX):**
- CONFIRMED: Dog friendly, riverside. Multiple 2025-2026 sources confirm dogs welcome.
- Note: This pub is on the NORTH (Twickenham/Middlesex) bank, not the south bank route. Only relevant to walkers using the north bank alternative. This should be clarified on the walk page - it is not on the standard Teddington-Richmond south bank route.
- Source: Research pack, Pure Pet Food "best dog-friendly pubs in Richmond" (purepetfood.com)

**The Dysart Petersham (Petersham Road, Richmond, TW10 7AA):**
- CONFIRMED WITH CAVEAT: Dogs confirmed welcome, water bowls and towels for muddy paws provided. However, the Dysart is now better described as a Michelin-starred restaurant rather than a pub. Simply dropping in for a drink without eating is discouraged. Dog walkers should note it is more of a post-walk dining venue than a casual pub stop.
- Source: TripAdvisor, SquareMeal, OpenTable

**The Waterman's Arms (research mentions this for this section):**
- UNVERIFIED: The Waterman's Arms (10 Broadway, Brentford, TW8 0LS) is near Kew Bridge, not on the Teddington-Richmond section. It appears in the research in the wrong section context. Do not include in Teddington-Richmond walk page content.

### Safety

**Petersham Meadows PSPO:**
- CORRECTED (repeated from Off-lead): PSPO = criminal offence to take dogs onto Petersham Meadows 1 April to 31 October. This must be a prominent safety flag on the walk page.

**Tidal limit:**
- NEW INFO: Teddington Lock is the tidal limit. The weir and lock area can be hazardous for dogs. Keep dogs away from the weir structure.

## Walk 3: Oxleas Wood and Shooters Hill (SE London)

**VERDICT: PASS WITH CORRECTIONS**

### Transport

**Parking - Oxleas Wood:**
- CONFIRMED: Free car parks off Shooters Hill Road. Main car park postcode SE18 3JA confirmed from multiple sources. Research states "Shooters Hill Road (SE18 area)" which is accurate. Two free car parks accessible from Shooters Hill Road.
- CORRECTED: Research pack (underrated-walks file) lists the Loughton car park (IG10 3SZ) as accessing the "Connaught Water loop" - this entry is under the Epping Forest section and correctly belongs there, not here. No error specific to Oxleas. For Oxleas: free parking confirmed.
- Source: Oxleas Wood official site, AccessAble, multiple visitor reports

**Public transport:**
- UNVERIFIED: Research pack does not specify public transport to Oxleas Wood in the sections reviewed. The nearest station options (Falconwood, rail - SE London lines; Welling; bus connections on Shooters Hill Road) are not verified from National Rail or TfL sources in the research pack. For the walk page, transport from Falconwood station (Southeastern Railway, zone 4) should be verified - it is approximately 10-15 minutes walk from the woods. This needs operator-source verification before publishing.
- NEW INFO: Bus route 89 and 486 serve Shooters Hill - this should be verified from TfL before inclusion.

### Off-lead

**Off-lead status:**
- CONFIRMED: Dogs welcome off-lead throughout the woodland and meadow areas. Royal Borough of Greenwich manages the site and confirms dog-friendly access. No formal lead requirements except near the cafe enclosure.
- Source: Royal Borough of Greenwich website (royalgreenwich.gov.uk), visitor reports, Friends of Oxleas Woodlands

**Cafe enclosure rule:**
- CONFIRMED: The Oxleas Wood Cafe is in a fenced enclosure that is dog-free (except guide dogs). Tables immediately outside the fence are available to dog owners. Staff serve outside tables. This is correctly described in the research pack.
- Source: AccessAble, multiple TripAdvisor and visitor reviews

### Livestock

**No livestock:**
- CONFIRMED: No livestock on Oxleas Wood. No lead requirements related to cattle or sheep.

**Ground-nesting birds:**
- UNVERIFIED: No specific ground-nesting bird restriction confirmed from Royal Borough of Greenwich for Oxleas Wood. Ancient woodland - ground-nesting bird risk is lower than in open grassland. Not a publishable restriction without authority source.

### Distance

**Walk distance:**
- CORRECTED: Research pack states "3-5 miles" for circular routes. More precise: AllTrails lists the Oxleas and Shepherdleas Woods Circular at 4.8 km (3 miles), Oxleas, Shepherdleas and Jack Wood Circular at 5.8 km (3.6 miles), and the Falconwood Circular (Oxleas Wood and Shooters Hill) at 6.3 km / 3.9 miles. The research pack range of "2-6 miles" is broadly accurate but should be tightened to "3-4 miles (5-6.5 km) for the standard circuits." The upper claim of "6 miles" in some research references is not supported by route data.
- Source: AllTrails (multiple Oxleas circuits), walkingclub.org.uk Falconwood circular PDF

### Bins

**Dog waste bins:**
- CONFIRMED: Dog waste bins present at main entry points to the woodland and in the cafe area. Royal Borough of Greenwich manages the site and maintains bins. Bins confirmed at main access points from Shooters Hill Road.
- Source: Greenwich council site, AccessAble accessibility guide for Oxleas Wood

### Water

**Drinking water for dogs:**
- CONFIRMED: Water available outside the cafe (staff serve outside tables). No confirmed dog water bowl tap separate from cafe service.

**Swimming / water access:**
- UNVERIFIED: No confirmed safe water access point for dogs within Oxleas Wood itself. The woods do not border a river. No pond access confirmed from managing body source. Research pack does not claim river access here. Advise no swimming available on this walk.

### Terrain

**Surface:**
- CONFIRMED: Ancient broadleaf woodland on varied terrain. Some slopes (Shooters Hill is elevated ground). Mix of natural woodland paths, some tarmac paths near cafe and car park. Can be muddy after rain.

**Hot weather paw risk:**
- NEW INFO: Tarmac paths near cafe/car park area could be hot in summer. Natural woodland paths are shaded and lower risk.

### Pubs

**Severndroog Castle Tea Room (Castle Wood, SE18):**
- CONFIRMED: Dogs welcome in the tea room on the ground floor and in the courtyard. Dog water bowls, treats, and dog ice cream on sale. Open Thursday to Sunday, Tea Room 9am-3:30pm; Viewing Platform 10am-3:30pm Sundays only.
- CORRECTED: Research pack describes Severndroog Castle as "the only dog-friendly castle in London" - this claim comes from The Londog blog. It is a marketing/editorial claim, not a verifiable fact. Do not publish as fact; it can be quoted as a popular characterisation if attributed.
- Source: severndroogcastle.org.uk, The Londog, TripAdvisor 2025-2026

**Oxleas Wood Cafe:**
- CONFIRMED: Open daily 8am-5pm. Hot meals, sandwiches, cakes, ice cream. Dogs outside the fence only (see above). Not dog-friendly inside.
- Source: livewellgreenwich.org.uk, AccessAble, multiple visitor reports

### Safety

**Cyclists in the woods:**
- NEW INFO: Oxleas Wood has mixed pedestrian and cyclist use on some paths, particularly near the cafe. Keep dogs under close control on the wider paths.

**Shooters Hill Road crossing:**
- NEW INFO: The A207 Shooters Hill Road bisects access to some sections of the woodland complex. Dogs should be on lead when crossing this busy road.

## Walk 4: Holkham Beach and Pinewoods (Norfolk)

**VERDICT: PASS WITH CORRECTIONS**

### Transport

**Parking - Lady Anne's Drive:**
- CORRECTED: Research pack states parking is "approximately £9 for a full day (2025 pricing)." This is INCORRECT. Current confirmed 2025 tariff from holkham.co.uk: up to 2 hours £3.60, up to 4 hours £7.00, over 4 hours / all day £13.50. The research pack figure of £9 is significantly wrong. The all-day charge is £13.50 not £9.
- CONFIRMED: Postcode NR23 1RG confirmed correct.
- CONFIRMED: Car park open 6am-9pm daily.
- Source: holkham.co.uk parking page (Car Park Tariff Update 2024), Parkopedia, See Around Britain

**Public transport:**
- UNVERIFIED: No rail station at Holkham. Nearest station is Wells-next-the-Sea (no longer has a rail service - the Wells branch line closed in 1964). The nearest operating station is Kings Lynn or Sheringham (Bittern Line). Bus access from Wells-next-the-Sea (Coasthopper bus service, route CH1). Research pack does not address public transport to Holkham - this must be flagged as a car-dependent destination with the Coasthopper bus as the only public transport alternative.
- NEW INFO: Coasthopper bus (Konectbus / Sanders Coaches) runs along the North Norfolk coast and stops near Holkham. This should be verified from the operator before publishing.

### Off-lead

**Beach off-lead (general):**
- CONFIRMED: Dogs welcome on Holkham Beach year-round. Over 70% of the beach remains off-lead accessible even during the restricted season (1 April to 31 August).
- Source: holkham.co.uk dog zoning initiative page, multiple confirmed sources

**Seasonal lead zones (beach):**
- CONFIRMED: 1 April to 31 August annually, dogs must be on leads in the most sensitive coastal nesting areas. A 90-hectare section around Holkham Gap and a 20-hectare Gun Hill section on the western edge are specifically roped off. Clear signage and beach wardens are in place.
- Source: holkham.co.uk dog zoning page, The Barking Bugle, Fakenham Times

**Pinewoods lead rule:**
- CORRECTED: Research pack states "Dogs must be on leads through the pinewoods at the Lady Anne's Drive entrance." This appears to be a year-round rule, not seasonal. Multiple confirmed sources (Holkham FAQ, walkiees.co.uk, Holkham website) consistently state dogs must be kept on leads when walking through the pinewoods - this is stated without seasonal qualification in official Holkham guidance. However, some sources link this only to the April-August period. The safest and most responsible position is: keep dogs on leads through the pinewoods at all times, as per the predominant official guidance. Publish as a year-round requirement.
- Source: holkham.co.uk FAQ, walkiees.co.uk Holkham dog walk page

### Livestock

**No livestock on beach:**
- CONFIRMED: No livestock on Holkham Beach. Not applicable.

**Ground-nesting birds:**
- CONFIRMED: The seasonal dog restrictions (April-August) are specifically to protect ground-nesting shorebirds including lapwings, ringed plovers, and oystercatchers breeding in the dune and beach transition zone.
- Source: holkham.co.uk dog zoning initiative, Barking Bugle

### Distance

**Walk distance:**
- CONFIRMED: Research pack describes "1 mile through pinewoods to the beach, then approximately 3 miles to Wells-next-the-Sea and 2 miles toward Burnham Overy." This is accurate. The car-park-to-beach walk through pinewoods is approximately 1 mile each way. The beach itself extends several miles in each direction. A standard out-and-back walk (car park, pinewoods, 2 miles along beach, return) is approximately 6 miles total. A car park to Wells-next-the-Sea circular is approximately 4.6 miles / 7.4 km (confirmed AllTrails and Countryfile).
- Source: AllTrails (Wells to Holkham beach circular, 15.8 km with detours; shorter routes 4-5 miles), Countryfile walk description

### Bins

**Dog waste bins:**
- CONFIRMED: Bins present at the car park and at the Lookout cafe. However bins are absent on the open beach itself. Advise carry out on the beach section.
- Source: Holkham estate information, visitor reports

### Water

**Drinking water - The Lookout cafe:**
- CONFIRMED: The Lookout cafe is dog-friendly with water bowls provided for dogs.
- CORRECTED: Research pack states The Lookout opens "daily 9:30am-5pm (to 7pm in summer)." Current confirmed hours: open from 10am-5pm (hours vary seasonally; mid-winter as low as 10am-3pm). The 9:30am opening time cited in research is not confirmed from official sources. Publish as "open from approximately 10am, hours vary by season - check holkham.co.uk before visiting."
- Source: holkham.co.uk opening times page, visitnorthnorfolk.com

**Beach water - sea swimming:**
- CONFIRMED: Holkham Bay is a wide, shallow tidal beach with generally safe conditions for dog paddling in calm conditions. However the bay is tidal - tide comes in quickly across the wide flat sands and can cut off walkers. NEW INFO flag: tidal risk - walkers should check tide times before venturing far from the car park across the beach. Incoming tide risk is a real hazard at Holkham.
- Source: General Norfolk coastal safety guidance; wellsguide.com

**Blue-green algae:**
- NEW INFO: No specific blue-green algae risk at Holkham beach (tidal saltwater location). Not applicable for saltwater beaches.

### Terrain

**Surface:**
- CONFIRMED: Broad sandy path through pinewoods (compacted, suitable for most dogs and pushchairs). Open sand on the beach. Some boardwalk sections through dunes.

**Hot weather paw burn risk:**
- NEW INFO: Sand at Holkham can become very hot in direct summer sun. In hot weather, walk dogs on the beach during cooler parts of the day. The tide-cooled wet sand near the shoreline is safer for paws.

### Pubs

**The Victoria at Holkham (NR23 1RG area, on the Holkham Estate):**
- UNVERIFIED: Research pack does not specifically mention The Victoria. This is the main on-estate pub/hotel directly adjacent to Lady Anne's Drive. Dog access not confirmed from 2025-2026 primary sources for the walk page. Requires direct verification.
- NEW INFO: The Victoria at Holkham (holkham.co.uk/stay/the-victoria) is on the estate and is a natural pre/post walk option. Dog policy should be verified from the official website before inclusion.

**Note:** The research pack does not name specific pubs for the Holkham walk section beyond the Lookout cafe facility. No unverified pub claims to correct.

### Safety

**Tidal risk:**
- NEW INFO (HIGH PRIORITY): Holkham Bay is a wide, flat tidal beach. The tide comes in very rapidly across the flat sand and can isolate walkers who have walked far from the shore access point. This is a known safety hazard. Walk pages must prominently advise: check tide times before visiting, do not walk across the tidal flats when tide is coming in, return to shore access before tide rises.

## Walk 5: Epping Forest - Connaught Water Circuit (Essex/East London)

**VERDICT: PASS WITH CORRECTIONS**

### Transport

**Parking - Connaught Water car park:**
- CORRECTED: Research pack (underrated-walks file) states "Loughton car park (IG10 3SZ) - access to Connaught Water loop." The Connaught Water car park postcode is E4 7QH (Rangers Road, Chingford), not IG10 3SZ. IG10 3SZ is a Loughton postcode that does not correspond to the Connaught Water car park. This is a meaningful error that would misdirect drivers.
- CORRECTED: Research pack describes Connaught Water car park as one of the "52 free car parks." This is INCORRECT for Connaught Water. The City of London Corporation now charges for parking at 36 designated car parks, and Connaught Water IS on the charged list. Charges: up to 1 hour £1.75, up to 2 hours £3.00, 2-4 hours £4.50, full day (over 4 hours) £7.00. Motorbikes free. Blue Badge holders free (registration required).
- Source: cityoflondon.gov.uk/car-parking-at-epping-forest (confirmed list names Connaught Water as a charged car park)

**High Beach car park:**
- CONFIRMED: High Beach Visitor Centre car park, IG10 4AF. Charged. Rates same as above.
- CORRECTED: Research claims "High Beach car park charges approximately £6 for up to 6 hours." Actual City of London charge for a full day (over 4 hours) is £7.00, not £6. For up to 4 hours it is £4.50. The £6 figure is not consistent with the published tariff.
- Source: cityoflondon.gov.uk

**52 free car parks claim:**
- CORRECTED: The research pack headline claim that "the forest has 52 free car parks" is outdated. The City of London introduced parking charges across 36 of the forest car parks. **Conservation grazing areas (cattle):**
- CONFIRMED: Cattle graze in Epping Forest as part of a conservation programme. GPS collar technology is used to contain cattle in specific zones. Dogs must not chase or disturb cattle. Dogs should be on lead near cattle at all times.
- Source: cityoflondon.gov.uk/grazing-in-epping-forest

### Water

**Connaught Water (the lake):**
- NEW INFO (SAFETY): Connaught Water has a documented history of blue-green algae outbreaks. In 2018, the City of London Corporation placed precautionary warning signs at Connaught Water following a suspected blue-green algae outbreak and asked the Environment Agency to investigate. Blue-green algae can be fatal to dogs. While the 2018 incident is historical, the risk recurs in warm weather at still water bodies. Dog owners must check the City of London's current notices before allowing dogs to enter or drink from Connaught Water, particularly in warm weather (July-September).
- Source: City of London press release (news.cityoflondon.gov.uk), general blue-green algae guidance

**Other water sources:**
- CONFIRMED: Forest has numerous streams and ponds beyond Connaught Water. The river at High Beach (not a navigable river - these are small forest streams) provides water access points.

### Terrain

**Surface:**
- CONFIRMED: Ancient woodland, mix of natural earth paths, some compacted gravel tracks. Can be very muddy October-March. The path around Connaught Water is hard and level (confirmed accessible). Forest paths away from the lake are natural woodland surfaces, can be uneven.

**Ancient pollarded hornbeam:**
- CONFIRMED: Epping Forest is one of the finest examples of ancient pollarded hornbeam woodland in Europe. SSSI, SAC and Special Protection Area designations apply.
