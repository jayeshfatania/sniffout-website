# Trail Tips Voice Guide - Jayesh (Founder)
Date: 13 April 2026
Status: Approved by owner

---

## Who is writing this?

Jayesh is the founder of Sniffout and owner of Luna,
a bearcoat Shar Pei from Kingston upon Thames. Trail
Tips are written in his voice - a knowledgeable,
experienced dog owner sharing things he has genuinely
discovered, not a blogger writing for SEO.

---

## The voice in one sentence

A smart, straight-talking dog owner telling a friend
something useful he has figured out - including the
bit that makes it clever, the workaround for when it
goes wrong, and the occasional aside about Luna.

---

## What to do

- Name the specific product or technique, not the
  category. "A squeezable bottle where the top flips
  into a bowl" not "a travel water bowl"
- Explain the mechanism - why it works, not just what
  to do. "The evaporation helps drop their body
  temperature" not just "wipe their paws"
- Give the tip a name. "The Wet Sock Cool-Down", "The
  Squeegee Method" - Trail Tips are discoveries, not
  instructions
- Include the real-world context. Luna is referenced
  naturally where relevant, not forced
- Include the backup plan or the thing that goes wrong.
  "If I forget the water bottle... I have used poo bags"
- Use parenthetical asides for colour. "(some nicknames
  given to Shar Peis are hippo face and meat mouth!)"
- Keep it short. One tip, one explanation, done. No
  preamble, no conclusion

---

## What not to do

- No em dashes
- No "it's important to remember" or "make sure you
  always" - just tell them
- No generic advice that appears on every dog blog -
  if it is on the Blue Cross website, it is not a
  Trail Tip
- Do not over-explain. Trust the reader
- Do not make it sound like a product review or an ad,
  even when a product is mentioned
- Do not start with "If you're a dog owner..." or any
  variation

## Tone benchmarks from Jayesh's own writing

"Tired of the vacuum not picking up stubborn hair from
the sofa and even clothes?"
- Direct, slightly wry, no preamble

"Any excess water is sucked back into the water bottle,
which helps preserve it for later"
- Practical, explains the clever bit

"I have used poo bags in the past, just roll it into a
smaller bag and add water"
- Honest about the improvised version

"The squeegee blade pulls up deeply embedded hair that
even the most expensive vacuums miss"
- Specific, confident, slightly surprising

"(some nicknames given to Shar Peis are hippo face and
meat mouth!)"
- Natural aside, warm, not trying to be funny

---

## What makes a tip a Trail Tip

- Something Jayesh has actually done with Luna
- Something most dog owners do not know or have not
  thought of
- Something specific enough to act on immediately
- Bonus if it has an affiliate product naturally attached

---

## Affiliate placement rules

- Only mention a product if it appears naturally in
  the tip itself
- Never add a product link that was not in Jayesh's
  original tip
- One link maximum per tip
- Frame as "I use X" not "buy X" or "check out X"
